## 🎴 Open Space Faclitation Card Game
A lightweight, multiplayer card game designed to spark better Agile conversations.
A simple way to get people talking about the things that actually matter.
Inspired by liberating structures and Open Space concept. https://www.liberatingstructures.com/25-open-space-technology/

## What is this?
The Open Space Card Game is a collaborative tool for running engaging discussions around:
Agile ways of working
Team challenges
Strategy and delivery
Or anything your group wants to explore
It combines Open Space thinking with a card-based format to guide, challenge, and sometimes disrupt conversations in a useful way.
Less “awkward silence in a workshop”
More “that was actually a great conversation”

## Why this exists
Because too many conversations look like this:
One person talks
Everyone else nods
Nothing really changes
This game nudges things in a different direction:
Encourages participation
Surfaces real topics
Keeps conversations moving
Gives people permission to challenge the room

## 🎮 How it works
Create or join a session
Each player gets a small hand of cards
A topic is revealed (or introduced)
Players can play cards to influence the conversation
That’s it. Simple by design.
## 🃏 Card Types
MOVE ON (ELMO)
Conversation is looping → time to move forward
NEW TOPIC (WOLF)
Bring something new into the room
REVERSE
Flip the perspective
REDRAW
Replace the current topic
## 🧩 Custom Topics
Players can introduce their own topics during a session with the WOLF Card (because the best conversations are usually the ones you didn’t plan.)
## 👑 Host Behaviour
The first person to enter becomes the host
If the host leaves:
The session ends
The room is marked as expired
Sessions can be resumed within 24 hours. After that, everything resets.
